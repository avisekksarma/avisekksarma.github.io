A comparative study of topic modeling methods for Nepali political news, evaluating probabilistic, algebraic, and transformer-based approaches in a low-resource language setting.

### Methods

We assess **LDA**, **NMF**, **LSA**, and **BERTopic** on curated Nepali news corpora, examining topical coherence, interpretability, and practical trade-offs between classical and neural techniques.
